Advantage Play is legal — but variance is real

Advantage Play (AP) is legal in most jurisdictions where slots are legal — you are not cheating, you are using publicly known information about machine mechanics. Results are not guaranteed. Even with a positive expected value, short-term variance can produce significant losses. A documented "+EV" play is not a winning play; it's a play where the math is in your favor over a large sample.
