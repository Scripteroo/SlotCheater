🔍 How the Mechanic Works

Money Galaxy is Konami's family of persistent-state coin-collection games (Pharaoh's Power, Radiant Witch, and others share the mechanic with different themes). Coin symbols accumulate toward a 3-level stand-alone progressive feature. When the collection threshold is reached, the bonus triggers — awarding credit prizes plus access to Mini, Minor, or Major progressives. After the bonus pays, the coin counter resets to its base level for that bet.

👀 What to Look For

Visible coin counter or meter on the screen showing collection progress

Higher progress = closer to trigger = more potential value to the next player

Check the bet level the previous player was using — the persistent state typically applies per bet level

Multi-cabinet bank scouting — check every cabinet, not just the most colorful one

Mini / Minor / Major progressive values — note the current displayed amounts

🛡️ When to Walk Away

Don't play if the coin counter is at or near base level — you're paying full cost-to-cover

Don't increase bet beyond the level the meter was built at — you may not get credit for previous progress

Set a hard walk-away point — if you've spent your estimated cost-to-cover and haven't triggered, leave

The bonus triggers and the meter resets — re-evaluate before continuing

📋 Banking Strategy Framework

1. Scout for visible meter progress. Walk the bank; look at every cabinet's coin counter before sitting.

2. Estimate remaining cost-to-cover based on observed progress vs the trigger threshold.

3. Verify bet level. Make sure your bet matches the level the displayed progress was built at.

4. Calculate +EV. Potential bonus value (credit prizes + progressives) vs cost-to-cover.

5. Walk away cleanly after a triggered feature pays — the meter resets, and you should re-evaluate before continuing.
