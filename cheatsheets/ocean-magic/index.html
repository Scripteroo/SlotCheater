🔍 How the Advantage Works

Unlike most slots, wild bubbles persist between players. They bubble up from below the screen after each spin.

When a previous player leaves with bubbles still rising, you inherit those wilds for free — they didn't pay for them, but you benefit.

Each spin, bubbles move up one row. Bubbles on the top row float off. Your goal: capture value before it disappears.

🎰 Game Basics

4 rows × 5 reels

One payline per credit bet

Two modes: Wild Bubble and Bubble Burst

🫧 Wild Bubble Mechanics

No wilds on reel strips — bubbles randomly appear from bottom after each bet

Wilds substitute for any symbol except scatter

If bubble replaces scatter, you get credit for both

Each spin: bubbles move up one row

Top row bubbles float off next spin

💡 Key: You can see bubble tops below bottom row — this is your edge!

✨ Logo Expansion

Bubble lands on Logo symbol → expands to all adjacent positions (8 wilds max)

Edge: 5 wilds • Corner: 3 wilds

Expanded wilds do NOT bubble up — only original bubble mechanics apply

Expansion on scatter = counts as both wild AND scatter

🎁 Bonus Game

3+ Treasure Chests trigger bonus

Stage 1: Pick bubbles → reveal 1-5 free games or 2-3 extra picks

Picks: 3 scatters = 5 • 4 scatters = 10 • 5 scatters = 20

Stage 2: Free spins on separate bonus reel strips

Can re-trigger • Max: 150 free games

💥 Bubble Burst Mode

Activated by doubling your bet

Half the bet = non-refundable fee

Wins based on half the bet with lower pay table

Randomly places extra bubbles ("exploding wild")

Burst bubbles also bubble up each spin

⚡ Industry Standard: Paid features typically have higher overall return when activated
