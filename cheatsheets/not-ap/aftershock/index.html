Aftershock / Aftershock Frenzy

Why this machine isn't on our advantage play list

🚫 NOT Advantage Play • Listed for Reference

🎰 What this game is

WMS 3-reel, 5-line slot with bars and sevens. Double and Triple Aftershock wild symbols multiply line wins.

The Aftershock Frenzy feature is randomly triggered and awards a series of high-multiplier spins.

The Aftershock Frenzy variant adds "big bet" spins where Triple Aftershock symbols can be upgraded for larger multipliers.

❌ Why it's not advantage play

There's no persistent state between players. The Frenzy feature triggers randomly with no visible "progress meter" to scout.

No must-hit-by progressive caps exist.

The game is pure variance — fun, but not exploitable.

For repeater volatility and multiplier feel: Hexbreak3r

For classic 3-reel AP patterns: Triple Double Diamond
