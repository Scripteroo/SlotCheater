Dragon Link

Why this machine isn't on our advantage play list

🚫 NOT Advantage Play • Listed for Reference

🎰 What this game is

Aristocrat's Dragon Link is a hold-and-spin family of games (Autumn Moon, Golden Century, Panda Magic, Genghis Khan, etc.) with four progressive jackpot tiers.

The hold-and-spin bonus triggers when 6+ coin symbols land on a single spin, then locks them and respins for additional coins/jackpots.

❌ Why it's not advantage play

Dragon Link has no persistent state. The Hold & Spin bonus is triggered by pure RNG — no accumulator, no meter, no cycle.

Despite its popularity, there is no way to gain a mathematical edge.

Do NOT confuse with Buffalo Link, which IS beatable thanks to its persistent Buffalo meter mechanic.
