Jacks or Better Playoff

Conditional advantage — only with a high pot

⚠️ CONDITIONAL AP

🎰 What this game is

Odyssey's Jacks or Better Playoff is a banking video poker variant. The machine accumulates a bonus pot over time — when the pot is high enough, qualifying hands trigger the playoff feature for an additional payout.

⚠️ Why it's only conditionally AP

The pot value is the entire edge. Only advantageous when the bonus pot contains 145+ credits.

Below that threshold, the game plays at standard video poker odds with no mathematical edge.

Check the pot display before sitting — it's visible on the cabinet. If it's low, walk past.
