🔍 How the Advantage Works

Scarab plays in 10-spin cycles. When scarab symbols land, they leave gold frames in that position.

On spin 10, ALL gold-framed positions turn WILD, then the game pays out and resets.

If someone leaves mid-cycle with frames on the board, you complete the cycle and collect their wilds!

🪲 Scarab Mechanics

Scarab symbol lands → creates gold frame in that position

Frames persist for remainder of 10-spin cycle

If scarab lands on already-framed position → nothing happens

Spin 10: ALL frames turn WILD → pays evaluate → cycle resets

Wilds substitute for all symbols except scarab and bonus

⚠️ Do NOT spin after the 10th spin payout! Advantage is gone — walk away.
