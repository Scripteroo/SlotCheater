🔍 How the Advantage Works

Reels expand from 3 to 8 rows when horseshoes land. Taller reels = more ways to win.

When reel reaches height 8, it hits Luck Zone → awards prize → resets to 3.

Reel heights persist between players! Find tall reels = close to prizes.

Black cat (reel 3) = PROGRESSIVE. Moves ~1 position per 1,900 spins!

🎰 Game Basics

5 reels • Heights vary 3-8 rows each

243 to 59,049 ways depending on heights

Pays BOTH directions (L→R and R→L)

5 bet levels (80-400 credits) • Each has separate state

Typical bet: $0.80 to $4.00

✨ Luck Zone Prizes

Above each reel at height 8 = Luck Zone orb

Reel 3: BLACK CAT = Progressive Jackpot! 🐱

Other reels: Credit prizes OR Bonus trigger

Horseshoe on height 8 → collect prize → reset to 3

💡 Reel 3 at 6+ means someone invested ~11,400 spins worth!

⚡ Reel Speed

Reels 2 & 4: Fastest — lower prizes

Reels 1 & 5: Medium speed

Reel 3: SLOWEST — ~1 per 1,900 spins

⚠️ Someone speed-tapping reel 3? They're AP chasing progressive. Move on.

🎁 Bonus Game

Trigger: 3 BONUS symbols (reels 2-4) OR hit Bonus orb

Unlimited free spins until skull

Guaranteed minimum 10x on any bonus
