🔍 How the Advantage Works

Each spin that lands buffalo symbols adds coins to the persistent buffalo coin meter.

When the meter fills, it triggers the Free Games bonus with enhanced multipliers.

The meter does NOT reset when a player leaves — this is the exploitable persistent state. You inherit whatever progress they walked away from.
