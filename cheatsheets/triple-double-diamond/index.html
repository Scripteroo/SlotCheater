🔍 How the Mechanic Works

Three meters above reels: Red (2×), Green (5×), Blue (10×)

Each meter must hit by 15 free games — guaranteed bonus!

Meters advance on fixed spin cycles (you don't see spin count)

When meter reaches 15 → automatic free games at that multiplier

Each bet level has separate meters — check them all!

After bonus, that meter's spin counter resets internally
