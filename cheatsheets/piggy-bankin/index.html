🔍 How the Advantage Works

There are 9 piggy banks with a total of 510 coins spread across them.

ONE random coin is the "lucky coin" — when captured, triggers the progressive pick bonus.

When 3x Wild symbols hit, coins are deposited into banks. Banks get eliminated as they fill.

Fewer banks remaining = fewer coins left to find = closer to the lucky coin!

🎰 Game Basics

3-reel classic slot machine

Available in 5-line and 9-line versions

Symbols: Multi-color 7s and 3x Wild

3x Wild multiplies line hits AND deposits coins

Two 3x Wilds = 9x multiplier!

🪙 Coin Mechanics

510 total coins spread across 9 banks

1 lucky coin triggers progressive bonus

3x Wild lands → deposits range of coins

Bank fills up → eliminated → fewer coins remain

Coin deposit amounts vary by limit (low vs high)

🎁 Progressive Pick Bonus

When lucky coin captured → bonus screen

Pick piggy banks to reveal coins

Keep picking until you reveal 3 coins connected to a progressive

Picks are predetermined ("for entertainment purposes only")

💡 Unlike some games, you KEEP picking until you win — guaranteed progressive!

⚠️ Key Considerations

3x Wilds come in clumps — can be streaky

Sometimes wilds won't come at all for long stretches

This is a risk/reward type game

Similar mechanic to New Year Festival (firecrackers)

⚠️ Can be volatile! Even with good position, wilds may take time.
