🔍 How MHB Progressives Work

Jackpot starts at floor ($9,000) and MUST hit by ceiling ($10,000).

Meter rises on coin-in. Typical: $250-$667 in = $1 meter rise.

Hits at random point between current and ceiling (uniform for Ainsworth).

If expected return > expected cost, you have +EV!

📊 Common MHB Ranges

Penny: Minor $20-$50, Major $350-$400

Dollar: Minor $350-$500, Major $9,000-$10,000

💡 Games: Mustang Money, Thunder Cash, Ming Warrior, Rumble Rumble

🧮 The Math

STEP 1: Find meter rate
Play $30 → Note meter movement
$30 → Major +$0.045 → Rate = $667/$1
STEP 2: Max coin-in needed
(Ceiling - Current) × Rate
($10K - $9,900) × $667 = $66,700 max
STEP 3: Expected loss
Max Coin-In × 15-20% house edge
$66,700 × 15% = $10,005 loss
STEP 4: Expected jackpot
(Current + Ceiling) ÷ 2
($9,900 + $10K) ÷ 2 = $9,950

⚠️ Example: Loss $10,005 > JP $9,950 = NEGATIVE EV!

💸 Bankroll Requirements

Calculate: Worst-case = meter goes to ceiling

Minor ($500): Budget $2,000-$5,000

⚠️ Horror stories: $35K+ lost on majors. Have bankroll or DON'T PLAY.
